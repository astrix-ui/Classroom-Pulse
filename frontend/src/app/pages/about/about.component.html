About Classroom Pulse

Real-Time Classroom Intelligence & Collaboration

Classroom Pulse is an interactive, synchronized classroom system built for instant question broadcasting, live student progress tracking, and dynamic group collaboration without the overhead of heavy learning management software.

Live Dynamic Pacing

Full-duplex WebSockets (STOMP over SockJS) broadcast questions to all student displays within milliseconds. Teachers maintain total control over problem sequence, instant inline question edits, and question retirement.

Real-Time Completion Lights

Instant live feedback matrix. Watch student status indicators shift dynamically between Pending and Done with animated pulses and percentage completion progress bars.

Collaborative Groups

Teachers configure target group sizes (2 to 10). Students dynamically form teams and select available peers. When any teammate completes the solution, answers seamlessly synchronize across the entire group.

Flexible Answering Modes

Support both speed-check simple mode ("I'm Done" one-click confirmation) and rigorous written response mode with automated clipboard protection to promote original student synthesis.

Answer Inspection Modal

Teachers can click any student row from the live roster to inspect their comprehensive session answer history, timestamps, and group affiliations in a clean, minimal inspection overlay.

Zero Overhead & Ephemeral

No permanent user accounts, sign-ups, or invasive tracking. Unique room codes connect educators and students instantly. All session state is clean, reactive, and ephemeral.
